Hören

Listening: School strike in San Francisco (ca. 45 Sekunden)

Hör den Dialog zuerst ganz an. Beantworte dann die Fragen. Kopfhörer sind erlaubt.

Transkript anzeigen

Maya: Did you see the news about the school strike in San Francisco?
Oliver: No. What happened?
Maya: A big demonstration was organized last Friday. More than two thousand students were invited by a group of young activists.
Oliver: Wow. How was the event publicized?
Maya: A video was uploaded on Monday, and it was shared thousands of times. Posters were put up all over the city, too.
Oliver: And what did the mayor say?
Maya: The students were welcomed by the mayor at the town hall. A petition was handed over, and it was signed by five thousand people.
Oliver: That is great. Will anything change?
Maya: Maybe. The petition is read by the city council next week, and the results are announced online.
Oliver: Then let us hope that the activists are heard.

Passive: be + 3. Form. present: am / is / are + 3. Form · past: was / were + 3. Form. Wer es getan hat, steht mit by.
