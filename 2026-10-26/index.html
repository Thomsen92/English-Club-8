Merke dir

Simple Past: regelmäßig -ed (visit – visited · try – tried · stop – stopped) · unregelmäßig = 2. Spalte (hang out – hung – hung) · Verneinung didn't + Grundform · Frage Did … + Grundform?

Relativsätze: who = Personen · which = Dinge · that = beides · where = Orte · whose = dessen / deren (Profi)

Vergleiche: cheap – cheaper – the cheapest · big – bigger – the biggest · expensive – more expensive – the most expensive · good – better – the best

Vergleichswörter: -er / more … than (als) · as … as (genauso … wie) · not as … as (nicht so … wie). Das Adjektiv bleibt bei as … as in der Grundform!
